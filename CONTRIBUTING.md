# Contributing to eduvia

## ⚠️ Project Status: Archived

**This repository is no longer actively maintained** by the original developers. Development and hosting were officially discontinued on **January 30, 2026**.

However, the codebase remains **open source** under the MIT License, and we welcome community forks and continued development!

---

## For Community Developers

### Want to Continue This Project?

If you'd like to fork eduvia and continue development:

1. **Fork the repository** to your own GitHub account
2. **Follow the setup guide** in [FORK_GUIDE.md](FORK_GUIDE.md)
3. **Make it your own!** Adapt, improve, and customize for your needs

### Not Accepting Pull Requests

Since this is an archived project, **we are not accepting pull requests** to the original repository. 

Instead, please:
- Fork the project and develop independently
- Share your fork with the community
- Credit the original team in your acknowledgments
